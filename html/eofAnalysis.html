shLowLevel.eofAnalysis

Empirical orthogonal functions of a coefficient series.

Syntax

[~, resid] = ts.climatology();
[modes, pcs, ve] = shLowLevel.eofAnalysis(resid, NModes=4);
modes{1}.map(Quantity="ewh", kn=kn)   % spatial pattern of mode 1
plot(resid.epochs, pcs(:,1))          % its temporal amplitude

Description

SVD of the time-centered coefficient stack. Mode k carries Uksk/√(T−1) as shCoefficients, PCS columns are unit variance, so field × pc reconstructs the mode exactly; VE are variance fractions. Tested: a synthetic two-mode field is recovered with > 99.9% variance in two modes and pattern correlation > 0.98.
