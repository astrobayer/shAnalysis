Class

shCoefficients

One spherical harmonic coefficient set (Stokes coefficients) as an immutable value class: I/O, arithmetic, standard corrections, filtering, spectral diagnostics, and spatial synthesis. The single point of access for per-field work; see shSeries for the time dimension.

Construction

g = shCoefficients.read("GSM-2_2024032-2024060_GRFO_UTCSR_BA01_0600.gfc");
g = shCoefficients(C, S, GM=3.986004415e14, R=6.3781363e6, Epoch=2024.12);
g = shCoefficients.fromVec(x, idx, gLike);      % from a shLowLevel.shIndex vector

read handles .gfc, .gfct and gzip-compressed files, parses GM/R/tide system from the header, and product type (GSM/GAA–GAD) plus mid-span epoch from standard GRACE L2 filenames.

Examples

gA = shCoefficients.read("GSM-2_202401.gfc").applyTN14("TN-14.txt");
gB = shCoefficients.read("GSM-2_202402.gfc").applyTN14("TN-14.txt");
d  = gB - gA;
[grid, lat, lon] = d.destripe.gaussian(300).synthesis(-89:89, 0:359, ...
                       Quantity="ewh", kn=kn);   % kn: user-supplied Love numbers
imagesc(lon, lat, grid*100); set(gca,'YDir','normal'); colorbar   % cm EWH
No load Love numbers or other physical tables are hardcoded anywhere in the toolbox — supply kn explicitly for EWH, and TN-14 tables as files.
