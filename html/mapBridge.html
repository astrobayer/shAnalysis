Handover to a map toolbox

shCoefficients.toGrid, shSeries.toGrid and shLowLevel.gridHandover — a synthesized field together with what it is.

Syntax

b = g.toGrid(lat, lon, quantity="ewh", kn=kn)          % one epoch
b = ts.toGrid(lat, lon, quantity="ewh", kn=kn)         % a stack, for animation
b = g.toGrid(lat, lon, quantity="geoid", LatType="geocentric")

Why it exists

g.synthesis returns three numeric arrays, and that is the right interface: numbers cross a toolbox boundary without either side importing the other. But an array of metres is an array of metres. When the interface was measured end to end, three things did not survive the trip, and each was something a user had to retype and could get wrong with nothing noticing.

What was lostConsequenceCarried now as
The latitude conventionA geocentric field drawn against a geodetic coastline: 0.1924 deg = 21.4 km at ±45, which is 1.6 mm of systematic shift on a regional mapb.LatType, and synthesis now returns the convention that was asked for
The unitsTyped by hand into the figureb.Units, from kernelFactors
The product typeA full field and a residual are both “m”, and one of them is unplottableb.ProductType, b.FieldKind, b.C00

The field kind is read off an identity

C(1,1) is the degree-0 coefficient: exactly 1 for a field normalised by GM/R that carries the total mass, and 0 for a residual. So "full" and "anomaly" are facts about the coefficients rather than inferences from the values, and anything in between is reported as "unknown" rather than decided. A stack whose epochs disagree is "mixed".

Measured: twelve GSM solutions meaned and synthesized to EWH give 1.16e7 m — the static field expressed as water, not a mass change — which a symmetric colour scale renders as a single flat colour. Asking for a mass quantity from a full field therefore warns (shCoefficients:toGrid:fullFieldAsMass). Asking for a geoid from one is legitimate and stays silent.

One colour scale across a whole animation

shSeries.toGrid returns b.Z as (nlat × nlon × nEpochs) and takes Zmin/Zmax over the whole stack. That is the point of building the stack in one call: an animation drawn with a per-frame colour scale shows the scale changing and the signal not, which is how 240 honest maps become a misleading film. b.Epoch lets the frames label themselves from the data rather than from a filename.

geoMap draws; this toolbox does not

As of v3.32.0 shAnalysis renders no maps. shLowLevel.plotSHMap is gone — it had its own coastlines, its own colour limits and two projections, beside sixteen certified ones next door, and no instrument compared the two. g.map and shLowLevel.writeAnimation now synthesize, hand over, and let geo.map draw.

The scope was measured before anything was deleted. shLowLevel.errorMap and shLowLevel.resolutionMap sound like renderers and contain zero graphics calls: they compute formal-error and resolution fields and return arrays. Both stay. The renderer was 114 lines in one file.

  • shLowLevel.requireGeoMap is the one place that checks for geoMap, so the dependency has one spelling and one error message — and that message shows how to draw the numbers without it. The dependency is checked, not required: shAnalysis still runs on base MATLAB, and only the two drawing entry points need geoMap.
  • shLowLevel.writeFrames writes a video container from ready-made RGB frames and contains no graphics. That split is deliberate: codec selection, extension validation, frame rate and the provenance sidecar are the parts that have bitten before, they never needed a renderer, and keeping them separate keeps them under test on a machine without geoMap.
  • tools/dev/no_map_renderer.py runs in CI and keeps the renderer gone. A rule enforced by review erodes.

geoMap's options are not restated here. CRS picks the projection, Basemap configures the raster element, and GeoArgs is forwarded verbatim. Mirroring another toolbox's option table is how two renderers drift apart in the first place.

Outputs

FieldTypeDescription
Z(nlat,nlon[,T]) doublethe synthesized values, unchanged — the bridge adds no arithmetic
lat, lon(nlat,1), (1,nlon) doubleas given, in the convention named by LatType
Units, Quantity, LatType, ProductType, Name, Source, FieldKind(1,1) stringSource is one line a figure caption can carry verbatim
Epoch, C00(:,1) doubleper epoch
Zmin, Zmax, Zmean, Nmax, Nmin(1,1) doublemeasured extremes, carried rather than judged

Example

ts = shSeries.fromFolder(folder);
res = ts.minus(ts.mean());
b = res.toGrid(-89.5:89.5, 0.5:359.5, quantity="ewh", kn=kn);
b.FieldKind        % "anomaly"
b.Units            % "m"

The struct's field names are chosen to feed a geo.grid-style constructor directly, so the handover is one line and no value is retyped.
