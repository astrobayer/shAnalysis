Class

shSeries

A time series of coefficient sets: everything with a time dimension. Mean field, climatology (bias/trend/annual/semi-annual), GAX background restoration, the classic destripe+Gaussian chain applied per epoch, the tvANS time-variable anisotropic Wiener filter, and basin averages with exact deconvolution. Immutable, with provenance in .history.

Construction

ts = shSeries.read("GSM-2_*.gfc");            % pattern; sorted by epoch
ts = shSeries.read(fileList, Truncate=60);    % string array of paths
ts = shSeries.fromFolder(folder, Truncate=60);   % every gfc file in a folder
ts = shSeries(objArray);                      % from shCoefficients array
ts = shSeries(Cs, Ss=Ss, Epochs=ep);          % raw (nmax+1)x(nmax+1)xT stacks

Mean over a user-given period v3.29.0

A mean field over a window — the 2004–2010 baseline that is subtracted before every trend analysis, a single hydrological year, the pre- and post-gap epochs of GRACE and GRACE-FO — is one call:

gBase = ts.mean(Range = [2004 2010]);              % decimal years
gBase = ts.mean(Range = [datetime(2004,1,1) datetime(2010,1,1)]);
dts   = ts - ts.mean(Range = [2004 2010]);         % anomalies about that baseline

Equal weights are correct only when the window is evenly covered. If months are missing, the arithmetic mean carries the trend and annual signal of whichever epochs survived: a single missing six-month block out of six years shifts it by 0.069 of a 1.2 annual amplitude, and the trend part is info.centroidOffset times the trend. The second estimator fits bias/trend/annual/semi-annual over the window and returns the exact time integral of that model:

[gBase, info] = ts.mean(Range = [2015 2021], Estimator = "model");
info.coverage        % fraction of the window actually sampled
info.maxGapYears     % largest gap between consecutive epochs
info.attenuation     % [period factor] per harmonic
info.centroidOffset  % epoch centroid minus window centre [yr]

With the reference epoch placed at the window centre the integral collapses to bias + Σk s(Pk)·cosk with s(P) = sin(πD/P)/(πD/P) and D = t2 - t1: the model at the window centre, each harmonic damped by a sinc factor. For a whole number of years s = 0 exactly, so the period mean is the trend line at the centre — whatever the phase of the annual signal and wherever the gaps sit. Sigmas are then formal (propagated through the fit), not scatter, and the call warns (shSeries:mean:sparseWindow) when coverage drops below 50% and the model is extrapolating rather than interpolating.

Voronoi time-weighting was implemented as a third estimator and rejected: it never won on the measured scenarios and lost by a factor of four across the 2017–2018 mission gap, because the two epochs bracketing a gap each collect half a year of weight. The table lives in tools/dev/validate_meanperiod.py.

Example: full GRACE-FO chain

ts  = shSeries.read("GSM-2_*.gfc", Truncate=60);
ts  = ts.restore(shSeries.read("GAD-2_*.gfc", Truncate=60));  % ocean bottom pressure back
dts = ts - ts.mean;
[clim, resid] = dts.climatology(Robust=true);
[tsF, op] = dts.filter("tvANS", SignalMode="inhomogeneous");
avg = tsF.basinAverage(B, Deconvolve=true, Op=op);            % K x T, unbiased
NaN-containing stacks are accepted for mean(Omitnan=true) but rejected loudly (shSeries:stack:nanInSeries) by the filtering chains — fill or drop months first (ts.dropNaN() since v2.1).
