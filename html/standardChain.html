standardChain — the canonical post-processing chain

The GRACE/GRACE-FO post-processing chain is short, but its order is not negotiable, and getting it wrong is the classic beginner error. shLowLevel.standardChain encodes the correct order as one entry point and returns a provenance record of what it actually did.

[ts, rep] = shLowLevel.standardChain(folder, Name=Value)

The five stages

#StageWhy here
1read the folder (shSeries.fromFolder)one series, epoch-sorted, gaps kept as gaps
2replace C20/C30 with SLR (TN-14)must happen on unfiltered coefficients
3restore degree 1 (TN-13)same reason; the geocenter is a coefficient like any other
4subtract a GIA trend (optional)a trend in the corrected coefficients
5filter (Gaussian, DDK, or your own W)last, because a filter mixes degrees
Why the order matters. A filter mixes degrees. If you replace C20 after filtering, you insert an unfiltered value into a filtered field, and the low degrees no longer mean anything consistent — the result looks fine and is wrong. Stages 2 and 3 therefore precede stage 5 unconditionally.

Typical call

gia = shCoefficients.read("ICE-6G_D_trend.gfc");   % a TREND field [1/yr]
[ts, rep] = shLowLevel.standardChain("D:/grace/monthly", ...
    TN14File = "TN-14_C30_C20_SLR_GSFC.txt", ...   % TN14 itself is a FLAG
    Degree1  = "CSR", ...                          % provider, not a path
    GIA = gia, Filter = "DDK3");
disp(rep.steps')

Three contracts that trip people up

OptionTakesNot
TN14a logical flag (default true)a path — that is TN14File
Degree1a provider: "GFZ" | "CSR" | "JPL" | "none"a path — that is Degree1File
GIAan shCoefficients trend field in [1/yr], applied as (t − GIAEpoch) × GIAa filename

Left at their defaults, the correction files come from the persistent data folder that setup_shAnalysis populates. Filter accepts "none", "gaussN" (N = radius in km), "DDKn" (read from the data folder — fetch it once with shLowLevel.fetchDDK), or a W struct from readDDK / designFilter.

The provenance report

REP is the record you paste into a paper's data section: files (every input), steps (the applied stages with their parameters), nmax, epochs, version (from shLowLevel.version at run time) and created. Skipped stages are reported as skipped rather than silently omitted — so a sensitivity study that turns off the GIA correction leaves a trace.

Feeding it a downloaded series

fs = shLowLevel.fetchICGEM(17, Type = "temporal");
[ts, rep] = shLowLevel.standardChain(fileparts(fs(1)), Filter = "DDK3");
