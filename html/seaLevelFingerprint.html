Sea level

shLowLevel.seaLevelFingerprint

Elastic sea-level fingerprint: the sea-level equation, iterated.

Syntax

idx = shLowLevel.shIndex(96, Nmin=0);
loadF = @(la,lo) -sigmaIce(la,lo);        % kg/m^2, negative = loss
[S, grid, info] = shLowLevel.seaLevelFingerprint(loadF, oceanMask, idx, ...
    kn=kn, hn=hn);
b = g.toGrid(grid.latDeg, grid.lonDeg, Quantity="geoid")

Description

The ocean does not rise uniformly: S = O(N − U + κ) with geoid change N and uplift U from the TOTAL load and κ enforcing global mass conservation (Farrell & Clark 1976, elastic limit), iterated on the exact Gauss-Legendre quadrature pair of the toolbox. Python-validated: mass conserved to machine precision, ~11 iterations, and the classic pattern — sea level FALLS in the near field of a melting mass (lost self-attraction + rebound) and exceeds eustatic in the far field (validation: near −0.3 mm vs far +0.8 mm about eustatic +0.6 mm per unit load).

Limitations (by design, documented): elastic only (no viscous GIA), no rotational feedback, fixed coastlines, band-limited masks (Gibbs at coasts — OverSample and Lmax control it).
