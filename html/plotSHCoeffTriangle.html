Diagnostics / plotting

plotSHCoeffTriangle

Degree/order 'butterfly' triangle image of log|Cnm| (left half) and log|Snm| (right half) -- the standard first diagnostic for spotting striping, leakage, or bad coefficients.

Syntax

plotSHCoeffTriangle(C, S)
plotSHCoeffTriangle(C, S, 'nmax', n, 'clim', [lo hi])

Description

Displays |Cnm| and |Snm| together as a single mirrored image, degree n on the vertical axis (n=0 at top), order m on the horizontal axis (Cnm to the right of center, Snm mirrored to the left). Because real geophysical signal is generally smooth in both n and m, patterns that break that smoothness are diagnostic:

  • Vertical banding at a fixed order (a column that is systematically brighter than its neighbors) is the classic signature of correlated noise / striping, and is what shDestripe targets.
  • Isolated bright spots at a single (n,m) with no smooth relation to neighboring coefficients usually indicate a bad or corrupted coefficient rather than real signal.
  • A broad bright wedge at high degree, low-to-mid order is typically GRACE/GRACE-FO's characteristic error growth with degree, motivating Gaussian smoothing.

Input Arguments

ArgumentDescription
C, S(nmax+1)x(nmax+1) Stokes coefficient matrices.
'nmax'Max degree to display, default size(C,1)-1.
'clim'[min max] log10(|value|) color limits; default auto-scaled.
'ax'Axes handle to plot into (default: new figure).

Output Arguments

ArgumentDescription
hThe axes handle used.

Examples

Basic triangle plot of a monthly gravity-change field

plotSHCoeffTriangle(dC, dS, 'nmax', 60);

Look for vertical banding at high order -- if present, destripe before proceeding to spatial synthesis.

Before/after destriping comparison

figure;
subplot(1,2,1); plotSHCoeffTriangle(dC, dS, 'nmax', 60, 'ax', gca); title('before');
[dCf, dSf] = shDestripe(dC, dS, 'minOrder', 6);
subplot(1,2,2); plotSHCoeffTriangle(dCf, dSf, 'nmax', 60, 'ax', gca); title('after');

The vertical banding at high order should be visibly reduced after destriping, without the low-order coefficients (real signal) changing noticeably.
