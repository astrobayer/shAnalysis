shLowLevel.errorMap

Analytic formal-error maps from a full coefficient covariance.

Syntax

out = shLowLevel.readSINEX('ITSG.snx', Output="covariance");
sig = shLowLevel.errorMap(out.M, out.idx, -89:89, 0:359, Quantity="ewh", kn=kn);

Description

Pointwise 1σ of the synthesized field: σ²(φ,λ) = a′Ma with the kernel- and Y-weighted row a. One Cholesky, then per-latitude assembly for all longitudes: exact, and the analytic cross-check of mcPropagate (tested against Monte-Carlo, 4000 samples, RelTol 0.12). PSD-singular covariances fall back to an eigenvalue square root. Cost O(nlat·nlon·P²): at n96 budget minutes for 1° grids, or truncate the covariance.
