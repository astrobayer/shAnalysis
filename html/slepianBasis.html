shLowLevel.slepianBasis

Spatially concentrated (Slepian) bases: the well-posed route to regional analysis.

Syntax

[G, lam, info] = shLowLevel.slepianBasis(idx, region)
[G, lam, info] = shLowLevel.slepianBasis(idx, region, NKeep=25)

Description

Solves the spherical concentration problem (Simons et al. 2006): eigenvectors of the localization kernel K = Y' diag(w.*mask) Y maximize the energy fraction λ inside the region. Columns of G (orthonormal, λ descending in [0,1]) span the region-concentrated subspace; the Shannon number N = sum(lam) = areaFraction * P counts the usefully concentrated tapers. Estimating only ~N Slepian coefficients replaces ill-posed regional least squares — the clean alternative to Kaula-regularized fits on regional grids.

Arguments

NameType / sizeDescription
idxstructshLowLevel.shIndex; P×P eigendecomposition — practical to Lmax ≈ 60–96
regionhandle | polygon | maskas in shLowLevel.basinKernel
NKeep(1,1) double, round(Shannon)number of tapers returned
G(P,NKeep) doubletaper coefficient vectors, G'G = I
lam(NKeep,1) doubleconcentrations, descending

Examples

Idx= shLowLevel.shIndex(60, Nmin=0);
[G, lam] = shLowLevel.slepianBasis(idx, @(la,lo) double(la>60));
a  = G' * shLowLevel.vecFromCS(g.C, g.S, idx);   % Slepian-domain coefficients
xR = G * a;                               % regional reconstruction
Validated: λ∈[0,1] to 4e-16, G'G−I ≈ 3e-15, trace identity exact (addition theorem). Step-by-step regional workflow: guide, “Regional analysis with Slepian functions”.
