I/O

shLowLevel.writeGrid

CF-style netCDF export of maps and map series (base MATLAB).

Syntax

shLowLevel.writeGrid('ewh.nc', grid, lat, lon, Name="lwe_thickness", Units="m")
shLowLevel.writeGrid('series.nc', stack, lat, lon, Name="ewh", Epochs=tYears)

Description

nccreate/ncwrite (base MATLAB), time as "days since 2002-01-01" like the mascon products, lat_type attribute, provenance stamp. Roundtrip-tested against ncread and readable by shLowLevel.readMascon, GMT, xarray, panoply.
