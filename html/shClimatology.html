Class

shClimatology

The deterministic seasonal model of a series — per-coefficient bias, trend, annual and semi-annual harmonics about reference epoch t0 — as returned by shSeries.climatology. The same model the tvANS filter separates internally, exposed as a first-class object.

Model

x(t) = bias + trend (t-t0) + cA cos 2π(t-t0) + sA sin 2π(t-t0)
            + cS cos 4π(t-t0) + sS sin 4π(t-t0)

Significance v2.1

The fit's per-coefficient 1-sigma uncertainties (OLS formula sqrt(resVar · diag(inv(A'WA))), Monte-Carlo validated to ratios 0.99–1.02) are stored in clim.coefSigma and attached to every component accessor's output: clim.trend().sigmaC etc. Typical use — a significance-masked trend map: synthesize the trend and its sigma separately and blank grid cells where |trend| < 2σ.

Examples

[clim, resid] = dts.climatology(Robust=true);
tr = clim.trend;                          % shCoefficients, per year
[gridTr, lat, lon] = tr.synthesis(-89:89, 0:359, Quantity="ewh", kn=kn);
[A, ~, ~, ph] = clim.amplitudeMap("annual", -89:89, 0:359, Quantity="ewh", kn=kn);
