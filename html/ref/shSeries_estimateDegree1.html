shSeries.estimateDegree1

Geocentre coefficients of every epoch, from the series.

Syntax

[d1, info] = shSeries.estimateDegree1(obj, ocean)[d1, info] = shSeries.estimateDegree1(obj, ocean, kn=..., OceanModel=..., LatDeg=..., ...)

Description

[D1, INFO] = ts.estimateDegree1(OCEAN, kn = KN) is shLowLevel.estimateDegree1(ts, OCEAN, ...): C10, C11 and S11 for every epoch by the method of Swenson, Chambers & Wahr (2008). That function documents the method, its grid and its outputs; D1 drops straight into ts.addDegree1.

Examples

From the help block
    d1 = ts.estimateDegree1(ocean, kn = kn);
    ts = ts.addDegree1(d1);
