shSeries.regionalKalman

The regional Kalman filter on this series, in a basis.

Syntax

[tsK, out] = shSeries.regionalKalman(obj, B)[tsK, out] = shSeries.regionalKalman(obj, B, ModelSeries=..., Order=..., Shrink=..., ...)

Description

[TSK, OUT] = ts.regionalKalman(B, ModelSeries = TSM) is shLowLevel.regionalKalman(ts, B, ModelSeries = TSM): this series' coefficients and sigmas are the observations, the process model is estimated from TSM in the basis B of ts.regionalBasis, and the filter and RTS smoother run on the basis coefficients. TSK is the smoothed series T a with sigmas through T; OUT carries the basis coefficients and, with kn, the EWH grid of the region and its basin mean with sigma. shLowLevel.regionalKalman documents the chain and its oracle.

Examples

From the help block
    B = ts.regionalBasis("Elbe", Type = "slepian", Threshold = 0.5);
    [tsK, out] = ts.regionalKalman(B, ModelSeries = tsm, kn = kn);
    [tsK, out] = ts.regionalKalman(B, ModelSeries = tsm, kn = kn, ...
        Background = tsHydrologyModel);
    [tsK, out] = ts.regionalKalman(B, ModelSeries = tsm, ...
        EstimateQScale = true);           % alpha from the data
