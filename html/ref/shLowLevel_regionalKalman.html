shLowLevel.regionalKalman

Kalman-smoothed regional gravity field in a local basis.

Syntax

[ts, out] = shLowLevel.regionalKalman(obsIn, B)[ts, out] = shLowLevel.regionalKalman(obsIn, B, ModelSeries=..., Order=1, Shrink=0, ...)

Description

[TS, OUT] = shLowLevel.regionalKalman(OBSIN, B, ModelSeries=TSM) is the single point of access to the regional Kalman filter of CLAUDE.md WP8: Kurtenbach's normal-equation filter (2012, DGK C-683, Sec. 3.3) and the RTS smoother on the coefficients a of the regional basis B of shLowLevel.regionalBasis, after Woehnke et al. (2025, RBF) and Steffen et al. (2026, Slepian). It chains the pieces the earlier rows built and changes none of them: 1. the process model in the basis from the model series TSM (shLowLevel.regionalProcessModel: climatology removed, every epoch projected onto B, VAR(Order) by Yule-Walker, Kurtenbach's alpha and ground noise on request) 2. one normal equation in the basis per epoch: the observation series' coefficients with their covariance (shLowLevel.regionalNEQ, kind "solution"), or the records the caller built - any kind of regionalNEQ, combined per epoch with shLowLevel.neqCombine 3. the forward filter in its normal-equation mode (shLowLevel.kalmanFilter) and the RTS smoother (shLowLevel.rtsSmoother) - together the joint least-squares adjustment over all epochs (Kvas 2019, Sec. 2.3; Python-validated in an RBF basis to 2e-15, gaps included) 4. the smoothed coefficients carried out through linear functionals F a whose sigmas come from the FULL smoothed covariance, sqrt(diag(F Ps F')): SH coefficients x = T a (the output series), EWH at the points of the region and the area-weighted basin mean of EWH. Formal sigmas of an shSeries are per coefficient, so the basin sigma could not be recovered from TS afterwards - this is where it is formed. On the SH basis (B.T = I) the chain IS shLowLevel.kalmanChain in solution mode: the same process model bit for bit, the same estimator - the information-form update agrees with kalmanChain's gain form to 5.6e-15 relative in the states and 8.9e-16 in the sigmas (tools/dev/validate_regional_kalman.py CLAIM 1). That is its oracle.

Examples

From the help block
    idx = shLowLevel.shIndex(60);
    B = shLowLevel.regionalBasis(idx, "Elbe", Type = "slepian", ...
        Threshold = 0.5);
    [tsK, out] = shLowLevel.regionalKalman(tsGrace, B, ...
        ModelSeries = tsESM, kn = kn, QScale = 10);
    errorbar(out.epochs, out.basin.mean, out.basin.sigma)

  Memory: the filter keeps two K x K covariances per epoch with the
  smoother; K is the number of basis functions, which for a region is
  far below the (nmax+1)^2 of the global chain.

AddNoiseCovn x 1double[](P,P) double solution mode: an SH covariance in B.idx order added to every epoch's noise covariance (the formal sigmas' or NoiseCov's; a scalar NoiseCov s is s I) - the error of a background model the series had removed, e.g. from shLowLevel.backgroundErrorCov; refused in record mode. Symmetric with a non-negative diagonal; the sum must be positive definite. Backgroundn x m-[](1,1) shSeries global background, nmax = B.idx.Lmax, with an epoch within Tolerance of every observation and every output epoch: removed from the observations (solution mode) and restored in every output, see above
