shLowLevel.maternCovariance

Non-stationary SPDE-Matern prior covariance of SH coefficients.

Syntax

[C, info] = shLowLevel.maternCovariance(idx)[C, info] = shLowLevel.maternCovariance(idx, PracticalRangeKm=..., Sigma=1, kn=[], ...)

Description

C = shLowLevel.maternCovariance(IDX, PracticalRangeKm = RHO, Sigma = S) is the covariance (P x P, IDX order) of the coefficients of the Gaussian field u of the stochastic PDE of Lindgren, Rue and Lindstroem (2011, Sec. 3.1-3.2), alpha = 2 (nu = 1 on a surface), on the sphere: (kappa(x)^2 - Laplace) (tau(x) u(x)) = W(x), W white noise. RHO and S are the LOCAL practical range [km] and the LOCAL pointwise standard deviation: scalars give the stationary Matern field, function handles f(latDeg, lonDeg) a non-stationary one - the form HB-STAR and R-INLA use, with log kappa and log tau given as fields. kappa = sqrt(8) / RHO (the correlation is about 0.13 at distance RHO in the plane), on the sphere of radius R.

Examples

From the help block
    idx = shLowLevel.shIndex(60);
    rho = @(lat, lon) 300 + 500 * (abs(lat) > 60);     % km
    C = shLowLevel.maternCovariance(idx, PracticalRangeKm = rho, ...
        Sigma = 0.02, kn = kn);                         % 2 cm EWH
    [tsK, out] = shLowLevel.regionalKalman(ts, B, ModelSeries = tsm, ...
        ProcessCov = C, EstimateQScale = true);
