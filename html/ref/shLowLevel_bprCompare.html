shLowLevel.bprCompare

A bottom-pressure recorder against an obpChain grid.

Syntax

rep = shLowLevel.bprCompare(obp, station)rep = shLowLevel.bprCompare(obp, station, Units="hPa", Tolerance=0.05, RhoWater=1000, ...)

Description

REP = shLowLevel.bprCompare(OBP, STATION) compares the series of one ocean-bottom-pressure recorder with the GRACE ocean-bottom-pressure field of shLowLevel.obpChain at the nearest ocean pixel (great-circle distance, across lon 0/360; NaN pixels are land). Both series are taken as anomalies about their mean over the epochs they share, in cm of equivalent water height; a recorder in hPa is converted with 1 hPa = 100 / (RhoWater g) m, 1.019716 cm for 1000 and 9.80665. WP9 9j. A recorder sees the full column, water and air, as obpChain's field does (GAD restored, nothing subtracted); its drift and tides must be removed before - that is the recorder's processing, not this toolbox's.

Examples

From the help block
    [out, ~] = shLowLevel.obpChain(folder, kn = kn, OceanMask = oc, ...
        GADFolder = gad);
    bpr = struct('lat', -57.5, 'lon', 295, 'epochs', t, 'value', p_hPa);
    rep = shLowLevel.bprCompare(out, bpr);
    fprintf("r = %.2f, NSE = %.2f\n", rep.correlation.r, rep.nse)
