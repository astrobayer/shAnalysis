shLowLevel.backgroundErrorCov

Background-model error covariance from two releases.

Syntax

[C, info] = shLowLevel.backgroundErrorCov(tsA, tsB)[C, info] = shLowLevel.backgroundErrorCov(tsA, tsB, Structure="full", Split=0.5, Shrink=0, ...)

Description

C = shLowLevel.backgroundErrorCov(TSA, TSB) estimates the covariance of the error of a background model - AOD1B, an ocean tide model, any product a GRACE solution has had removed and whose error is not in the solution's formal covariance - from two releases or two models of it, TSA and TSB, at their common epochs. The difference d_t = a_t - b_t holds the two errors and none of the signal; with the mean removed and the two errors independent and of equal size, E[d d'] = 2 C, so C = Split * S_d, Split = 1/2, S_d the sample covariance of the differences (divisor T - 1; T with RemoveMean = false, where no mean is estimated). The two series must share GM and R, hold only finite coefficients, and pair one-to-one: an epoch of either within Tolerance of two of the other is refused. Add C to the observations' noise covariance - shLowLevel.regionalKalman's AddNoiseCov does that at every epoch.

Examples

From the help block
    % two AOD1B releases' monthly means, as shSeries, same months
    C = shLowLevel.backgroundErrorCov(tsAODrl06, tsAODrl07, Shrink = 0.1);
    [tsK, out] = ts.regionalKalman(B, ModelSeries = tsm, AddNoiseCov = C);
