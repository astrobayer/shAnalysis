shSeries.bridgeGaps

Fill the months this series lacks from a second series.

Syntax

[out, info] = shSeries.bridgeGaps(obj, bridge)[out, info] = shSeries.bridgeGaps(obj, bridge, RadiusKm=..., Tolerance=..., MinOverlap=...)

Description

[OUT, INFO] = ts.bridgeGaps(TSB) is shLowLevel.bridgeGaps(ts, TSB): it fills every month ts lacks within its span from the series TSB, calibrated on the months both have - an offset, a per-degree scale and a per-degree sigma - and names each filled month "bridge:" plus its name in TSB. Both series are truncated to the lower nmax and smoothed with a 750 km Gaussian unless told otherwise; shLowLevel.bridgeGaps documents the method, what it was measured to do on GRACE and HLSST, and why TN-14 comes first.

Examples

From the help block
    ts = ts.applyTN14(tnFile);
    [tsF, info] = ts.bridgeGaps(hlsst);
