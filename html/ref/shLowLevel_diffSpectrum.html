shLowLevel.diffSpectrum

Spectral comparison of two coefficient sets.

Syntax

spec = shLowLevel.diffSpectrum(C1, S1, C2, S2)

Description

SPEC = shLowLevel.diffSpectrum(C1, S1, C2, S2) compares two solutions in the spectral domain: the difference degree amplitude localizes the disagreement in wavelength, the degree correlation separates "same pattern, different amplitude" from genuine disagreement, and the crossover of the difference against the signal spectrum gives the effective resolution of agreement as a single scalar. Inputs must share the same size; truncate beforehand for mixed degrees (the shLowLevel.compareSolutions wrapper does this automatically).

Examples

From the help block
    gF = g.gaussian(350);
    spec = shLowLevel.diffSpectrum(g.C, g.S, gF.C, gF.S);
    semilogy(spec.n, [spec.amp1, spec.diffAmp]); grid on

Name-Value Arguments

None.

Output Arguments

NameDescription
spec(1,1) struct fields: .n (nmax+1 x 1) double degree axis 0..nmax .diffAmp (nmax+1 x 1) double degree amplitude of (1)-(2) .amp1 (nmax+1 x 1) double signal degree amplitude of (1) .amp2 (nmax+1 x 1) double signal degree amplitude of (2) .degCorr (nmax+1 x 1) double per-degree correlation (NaN where a degree is all-zero) .ncross (1,1) double first degree where diffAmp exceeds amp1 (NaN if never; agreement to nmax)
