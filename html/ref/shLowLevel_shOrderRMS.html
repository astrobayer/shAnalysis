shLowLevel.shOrderRMS

Order-domain spectral diagnostics (striping direction).

Syntax

spec = shLowLevel.shOrderRMS(C, S)spec = shLowLevel.shOrderRMS(C, S, R=6378136.3, Nmin=0, SigmaC=[], ...)

Description

SPEC = shLowLevel.shOrderRMS(C, S) sums coefficient power per ORDER m - the complementary view to shLowLevel.shDegreeRMS and the natural axis for GRACE striping (correlated errors live at high orders):

Examples

Runnable (docs/apiExamples.json)
spec = shLowLevel.shOrderRMS(g.C, g.S);           % stripes live at high orders
