shCoefficients.applyDDK

Apply a DDK (order-block-diagonal) decorrelation filter.

Syntax

out = shCoefficients.applyDDK(obj, W)

Description

OUT = obj.applyDDK(W) with W from shLowLevel.readDDK (struct, .mat, or documented ASCII exchange format). Degrees not covered by the filter pass through unchanged; sigmas are NOT propagated through the filter (set to NaN - use shLowLevel.mcPropagate for filtered uncertainties).

Examples

Runnable (docs/apiExamples.json)
g = g.applyDDK("DDK3");
