shLowLevel.readMascon

Read a GRACE/GRACE-FO mascon netCDF (JPL / CSR / GSFC style).

Syntax

mas = shLowLevel.readMascon(filename)

Description

MAS = shLowLevel.readMascon(FILENAME) reads gridded mascon solutions for comparison against the SH chain (base MATLAB: ncread/ncinfo, no toolboxes). Variable names are auto-detected among the layouts used by JPL RL06 ('lwe_thickness'), CSR RL06 ('lwe_thickness'), and generic 'lwe'/'water_thickness'; CF time units "<days | hours | minutes | seconds> since Y-M-D[ h:m[:s]]" (one- or two-digit month, day and clock fields) are converted to decimal years.

Examples

Runnable (docs/apiExamples.json)
M = shLowLevel.readMascon("CSR_..._Mascons.nc");   % lat/lon/time EWH stack
