shLowLevel.seaLevelBudget

Ocean mass against altimetry minus steric sea level.

Syntax

rep = shLowLevel.seaLevelBudget(mass, total, steric)rep = shLowLevel.seaLevelBudget(mass, total, steric, Tolerance=0.05)

Description

REP = shLowLevel.seaLevelBudget(MASS, TOTAL, STERIC) closes the sea- level budget on the epochs the three series share: GRACE ocean mass (shLowLevel.oceanChain's oceanMean, converted to the unit of the others), total sea level from altimetry and the steric part from Argo, all as global or basin means. The residual r = total - steric - mass and the two sides mass and total - steric are fitted with bias, trend, annual and semi-annual terms (shLowLevel.fitDeterministicModel, ARCorrect, T0 = 2000.0), and the budget CLOSES when |trend(r)| <= 2 sigma(trend(r)), sigma AR(1)-corrected. The three fits share one design, so trend(r) is trend(total - steric) - trend(mass) exactly. WP9 9j; the published budget (WCRP Global Sea Level Budget Group 2018 and its updates) is the real-data check, run where the products are.

Examples

From the help block
    [oc, ~] = shLowLevel.oceanChain(folder, kn = kn, OceanMask = oc);
    mass = struct('epochs', oc.epochs, 'value', 10 * oc.oceanMean);  % mm
    rep = shLowLevel.seaLevelBudget(mass, gmsl, argo);
    tr = rep.trend; sg = rep.trendSigma;               % mass, sides, residual
    fprintf("residual %.2f +- %.2f mm/yr\n", tr(3), sg(3))
