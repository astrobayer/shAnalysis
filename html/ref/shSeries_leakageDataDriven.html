shSeries.leakageDataDriven

Model-free leakage correction of a catchment mean.

Syntax

[fHat, info] = shSeries.leakageDataDriven(obj, latDeg, lonDeg)[fHat, info] = shSeries.leakageDataDriven(obj, latDeg, lonDeg, Filter=..., Quantity="ewh", kn=[], ...)

Description

[FHAT, INFO] = ts.leakageDataDriven(LAT, LON, Mask = R, Filter = F, kn = KN) synthesizes every epoch on the grid (g.synthesis, geocentric, Quantity "ewh" unless told otherwise) and runs shLowLevel.leakageDataDriven on the stack with the series' GM and R - Vishwakarma's method of deviation, or with Method = "scale" the method of scale; both repair the catchment average from the filtered fields alone. FHAT is the corrected catchment mean, one value per epoch. shLowLevel.leakageDataDriven documents the method, the catchment size it is designed for (about 65000 km^2 and up), and why it needs at least 12 epochs and no NaN.

Examples

From the help block
    [f, info] = ts.leakageDataDriven(lat, lon, Mask = basin, ...
        Filter = "gauss300", kn = kn);
