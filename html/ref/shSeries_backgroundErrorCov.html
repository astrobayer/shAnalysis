shSeries.backgroundErrorCov

Background-model error covariance from two releases.

Syntax

[C, info] = shSeries.backgroundErrorCov(obj, other)[C, info] = shSeries.backgroundErrorCov(obj, other, Structure=..., Split=..., Shrink=..., ...)

Description

[C, INFO] = tsA.backgroundErrorCov(TSB) is shLowLevel.backgroundErrorCov(tsA, TSB): the error covariance of a background product (AOD1B, an ocean tide model) estimated from the differences of two of its releases at their common epochs, in shLowLevel.shIndex(nmax, Nmin = Nmin) order. Pass it to ts.regionalKalman as AddNoiseCov. That function documents the estimator, its limits and INFO.

Examples

From the help block
    C = tsAOD06.backgroundErrorCov(tsAOD07, Shrink = 0.1);
