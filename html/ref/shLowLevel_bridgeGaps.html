shLowLevel.bridgeGaps

Fill the months a series lacks from a second, noisier series.

Syntax

[out, info] = shLowLevel.bridgeGaps(ref, bridge)[out, info] = shLowLevel.bridgeGaps(ref, bridge, RadiusKm=750, Tolerance=0.05, MinOverlap=24)

Description

OUT = shLowLevel.bridgeGaps(REF, BRIDGE) fills every month that the reference series REF lacks within its own span, and that BRIDGE has, calibrating BRIDGE against REF on the months both have. The intended pair is a GRACE / GRACE-FO series and a high-low SST + SLR series such as QuantumFrontiers HLSST_SLR_COMB2023s, whose months cover the gap between the two missions (2017-07 to 2018-05) and the scattered months GRACE lost to battery management.

Examples

From the help block
    gr = shSeries.read("GSM-2_*.gfc").applyTN14(tnFile);
    hl = shSeries.read(fullfile(hlFolder, "kalman", "*.gfc"));
    [ts, info] = shLowLevel.bridgeGaps(gr, hl);
    filled = ts.epochs(info.bridged);

Name-Value Arguments

NameSizeTypeDefaultDescription

Tolerance1 x 1double0.05two months match when their epochs differ by less [yr]
MinOverlap1 x 1double24the fewest matched months the calibration accepts

Output Arguments

NameDescription
out(1,1) shSeries REF's months and the filled ones, sorted by epoch, truncated and smoothed as above
info(1,1) struct fields bridged (T,1) logical true for the filled months of OUT overlap (1,1) double months the calibration used scale (nmax+1,1) double s_n; 0 where BRIDGE's anomaly has no variance at that degree sigma (nmax+1,1) double the fill's sigma per degree offsetC (nmax+1 x nmax+1) double B0 - R0, cosine terms offsetS (nmax+1 x nmax+1) double B0 - R0, sine terms radiusKm (1,1) double the Gaussian radius used
