shSeries.combineCenters

Combine this series with other centres' by VCE.

Syntax

[tsComb, info] = shSeries.combineCenters(obj, others)[tsComb, info] = shSeries.combineCenters(obj, others, Tolerance=..., AllowMissing=..., Robust=..., ...)

Description

[TSCOMB, INFO] = ts.combineCenters({TSB, TSC}) is shLowLevel.combineCenters({ts, TSB, TSC}): one variance factor per centre and month, by variance-component estimation, on the epochs of this series. That function documents the method and what its posterior sigmas can and cannot claim.

Examples

From the help block
    [ts, info] = tsITSG.combineCenters({tsCSR, tsGFZ});
