shSeries.excitation

Earth-rotation excitation of the series' degree-2 terms.

Syntax

[tbl, info] = shSeries.excitation(obj)[tbl, info] = shSeries.excitation(obj, Earth=..., kn=..., TN14=[], ...)

Description

[TBL, INFO] = ts.excitation(Earth = E, kn = KN) runs shLowLevel.rotationExcitation on the series' C21 and S21 - Chen & Wilson (2003) eq. (1) solved for the excitation - and returns one row per epoch: t [yr], chi1 and chi2 [mas]. shLowLevel.rotationExcitation documents the equation, the frame of chi2 (towards 90 E, not the IERS pole's 90 W) and the Earth's constants (IERS 2010 unless given).

Examples

From the help block
    tbl = ts.excitation(kn = kn, TN14 = tnFile);
