shSeries.mean

Mean field, over the whole series or a user-given window.

Syntax

[g, info] = shSeries.mean(obj)[g, info] = shSeries.mean(obj, Range=[], Estimator="arithmetic", Periods=double.empty(1,0), ...)

Description

G = ts.mean() averages every epoch and returns an shCoefficients whose sigmas are the standard error of the temporal scatter, divided by the number of VALID epochs per coefficient (before v3.29.0 it divided by nEpochs, which understated the sigmas of any series holding NaN months).

Examples

From the help block
    gBase = ts.mean(Range = [2004 2010]);
    [gGap, info] = ts.mean(Range = [2015 2021], Estimator = "model");
Runnable (docs/apiExamples.json)
gM = ts.mean;                          % NaN-month tolerant
