shCoefficients.subtractNormalField

Remove the ellipsoidal normal field.

Syntax

out = shCoefficients.subtractNormalField(obj)out = shCoefficients.subtractNormalField(obj, System="WGS84", GM=NaN, a=NaN, ...)

Description

OUT = g.subtractNormalField() % WGS84 OUT = g.subtractNormalField(System ("WGS84")="GRS80") Computes the even zonals from the DEFINING constants (shLowLevel.normalFieldCS, Heiskanen-Moritz closed form; validated against NIMA TR8350.2 to all published digits), rescales them from the ellipsoid's own (GM (NaN), a (NaN)) to the object's (GM, R) - the WGS84 GM and a DIFFER from the ICGEM values, skipping this step costs millimetres - and subtracts them including the degree-0 GM ratio term. The result is the disturbing field T: 'geoid' synthesis then yields proper +-100 m undulations (Bruns).

Examples

Runnable (docs/apiExamples.json)
g = g.subtractNormalField(System = "GRS80");
