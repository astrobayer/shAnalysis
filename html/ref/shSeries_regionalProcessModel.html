shSeries.regionalProcessModel

Kurtenbach's process model of this series in a basis.

Syntax

[model, info] = shSeries.regionalProcessModel(obj, B)[model, info] = shSeries.regionalProcessModel(obj, B, Order=..., Shrink=..., Structure=..., ...)

Description

[MODEL, INFO] = ts.regionalProcessModel(B) is shLowLevel.regionalProcessModel(ts, B): the climatology of this model series is removed, every epoch projected onto the basis B of ts.regionalBasis, and the VAR process model of Kurtenbach (2012) estimated from the basis coefficients. That function documents the method and its two adjustments (QScale, the alpha of his eq. 4.8, and GroundNoise).

Examples

From the help block
    B = tsModel.regionalBasis("Elbe");
    [model, info] = tsModel.regionalProcessModel(B, QScale = 10);
